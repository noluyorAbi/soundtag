# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This is a small project about making objects out of songs, and there is no reason for anyone to have a bad time here.

## What is expected

- Assume the other person is trying to help
- Criticise the work, not the person. "This mesh is not manifold and here is the file" is useful; "this is garbage" is not
- Accept that the maintainer may say no to a feature, and say why
- Respect that people arrive with different printers, different slicers and different amounts of experience

## What is not accepted

Harassment, personal attacks, sexualised language or imagery, publishing someone's private information, or sustained disruption of discussions. Reports of the above are handled by the maintainer.

## Reporting

Open a private report through GitHub's report abuse flow, or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile at [@noluyorAbi](https://github.com/noluyorAbi). Reports are read, and the person who reported is told what happened.

The maintainer may remove comments, close issues, and block accounts, and will say when it has been done and why.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and anywhere someone represents the project.
